The Rock: Fundamentals

Master the foundational groove of "The Rock" focusing on weight transfer and relaxed posture. Remember to keep your knees soft.

Key Takeaways

  • The "Rock" originates from early breaking movements and mimics a rocking chair motion.
  • Focus on shifting your weight completely from the front leg to the back leg.
  • Keep your upper body relaxed; tension kills the groove.
