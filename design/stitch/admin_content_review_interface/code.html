The "Dougie - Variation B" emphasizes a smoother shoulder roll compared to the classic iteration. Start with a wide stance, knees slightly bent.

  1. Count 1-2: Lean right, brush left hand over head (comb the hair motion).
  2. Count 3-4: Shift weight left, right shoulder dip.
  3. Count 5-6: Slide right foot in, pop chest.
  4. Count 7-8: Freestyle transition or reset to neutral.

Ensure the core remains engaged throughout the shift to maintain balance during the lean.
