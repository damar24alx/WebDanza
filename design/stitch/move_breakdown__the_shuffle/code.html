The Shuffle

Intermediate Footwork Category 5 min practice
HD 1080p
0:37 / 2:23
footprint

Step-by-Step Technique

1

The Lift

0:15

Lift your right knee up towards your waist, keeping your foot relaxed. Simultaneously, ensure your weight is balanced on your left foot.

2

The Hop & Kick

0:20

Hop slightly on your left foot while kicking your right foot downwards and slightly forward. The movement should be sharp.

3

The Reset

0:25

Slide your left foot back to center as you bring your right foot down to the floor, preparing to switch legs immediately.

warning

Common Mistakes

cancel Mistake: Leaning Back

Leaning too far back throws off your center of gravity, making quick transitions impossible.

check_circle Fix: Keep chest over toes and engage your core.
cancel Mistake: Heavy Feet

Stomping or landing flat-footed reduces speed and increases impact on joints.

check_circle Fix: Stay on the balls of your feet ("demi-pointe").
