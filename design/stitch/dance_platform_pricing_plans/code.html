Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch from a Style Pack to Pro later? expand_more
Absolutely. You can upgrade to the Pro plan at any time from your account settings. We will prorate the remaining value of your current subscription towards the upgrade.
Do certificates expire? expand_more
No, once earned, your certificates are yours forever. You can download them as PDFs or share the unique verification link on LinkedIn indefinitely.
What styles are included in "Urban Family"? expand_more
The Urban Family pack includes Hip Hop, Breaking, Popping, Locking, Krump, and House Dance. It's one of our most comprehensive collections.
Can I download videos to watch offline? expand_more
Yes! Offline viewing is available on our iOS and Android mobile apps for both the "Pack por Estilo" and "Todo Acceso (Pro)" plans.
